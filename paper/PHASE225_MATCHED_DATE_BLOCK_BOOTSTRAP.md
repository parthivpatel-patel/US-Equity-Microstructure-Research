# Phase 225: Matched security/exchange contrasts and date-block sensitivity

This independent research checkpoint adds a one-to-one matched-security contrast with common trading dates and exchanges, plus a seeded circular moving-block bootstrap resampled **separately within** the two pre-reform windows. The method averages matched differences equally over pairs and exchanges, then contrasts the mean of the late window against the early window. Every required security × exchange × session cell must be present with valid positive execution counts. This intentionally refuses imputation and is sensitive to complete-case selection.

Eight synthetic regression tests passed locally on November 7, 2026 against the equivalent standalone module. The constructed four-session example has a constant matched contrast of 0.1 and therefore zero late-minus-early difference. A separate constructed sequence [0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8] has a difference of 0.5. Neither is an SEC observation. The published test uses the repository import path; clean-checkout execution of these exact published bytes remains outstanding.

**Important inference limitation:** Resampling blocks independently within early and late windows preserves within-window local dependence but **does not preserve covariance across the window boundary**. Its percentile endpoints are descriptive sensitivity diagnostics, not an authenticated empirical confidence interval and not a substitute for the Phase 224 full-series HAC, two-way clustering, security-level resampling or matched-group uncertainty. The method assumes exchange and pair rosters are supplied and does not authenticate the matching algorithm, market calendar, source records or historical Nasdaq treatment assignment. The percentile index is a simple empirical order statistic without interpolation or bias correction.

The October 2025 placebo warning, September pretrends, denominator/equal-cell weighting divergence, treatment reconstruction, SEC provenance and missing-cell selection remain unresolved. Original historical research ZIPs and licensed datasets were not available for authenticated import. This checkpoint imports none and generates no empirical figures. No causal effect is established.
