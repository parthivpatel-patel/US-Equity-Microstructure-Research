# Phase 430 — Common-support coverage gate (2026-11-17)

Reviewed the published Phase 421 September pretrend implementation and Phase 429 inference audit. The Phase 421 estimator averages treated-minus-control rates over paired (date, match_group, exchange) cells, but its date-by-date set of paired groups can change. Consequently, a September slope may reflect changing cell composition rather than within-unit evolution. Its descriptive slope should not be interpreted as a fixed-composition pretrend.

A new local prototype `phase430_common_support.py` audits paired cells separately before and after 2025-11-03 and counts group-exchange units paired in both periods. It rejects duplicate security/exchange/date records, unstable treatment/group assignments, invalid execution counts, blank identifiers and weekend dates. Seven deterministic unit tests passed locally, covering common support, no common support, unpaired cells, duplicates, unstable assignments, invalid counts and weekends. The prototype and tests are retained locally but **not yet published in the repository**; the exact checkout has not been executed. No historical SEC data were used and no historical estimates were generated.

Crucial limitation: a group-exchange unit appearing in both periods does **not** establish the same security composition or common trading dates. Fixed-security support and date-block/matched-group dependence-aware inference require additional gates. The substantial October placebo, September pretrends, selection/weighting sensitivity, and treatment/source authentication remain unresolved. The November 14 publication deadline was missed; manuscript not publication-certified. No historical ZIP imported this session. Strictly independent of GIG and other projects.
