* Phase 140: all eight reported interval widths exactly equal upper minus lower endpoints from Phase 139 at six displayed decimals: count widths 0.215569, 0.215045, 0.246308, 0.252172; volume widths 0.205383, 0.195718, 0.194962, 0.195387.
* Phase 135 volume pre-reform HAC(5) slope is −0.0272755292407 pp/day, interval [−0.045157977424, −0.00939308105748]. Its reported interval excludes zero. Count slope is −0.00408250981954 pp/day, interval [−0.0222116765723, 0.0140466569332], which includes zero. These are *reported* presentation results, not independently re-estimated coefficients.

## Interpretation and limitations
The November count result and volume result behave very differently in the published placebo comparisons. The strong October slope shifts for both outcomes are **pre-reform**, and are a serious identification warning, not supportive evidence of a November causal effect. The 78 historical placebo comparisons can overlap, and their proportions **must not** be treated as valid independent randomization p-values. Block bootstrap intervals from summary tables cannot establish that group and calendar-date dependence, fixed-roster selection, security/exchange controls, and serial dependence were all handled correctly. The Phase 135 HAC presentation alone cannot authenticate underlying dates or source data. Different outcomes, estimands, cutoffs and windows cannot be casually pooled.
