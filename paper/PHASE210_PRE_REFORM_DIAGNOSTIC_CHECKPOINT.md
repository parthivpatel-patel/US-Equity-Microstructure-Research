Tests covered linear and constant series, overlapping windows, noncontiguous dates, duplicate/unsorted dates, NaN values, reform-date intrusion, short windows, and missing dates. These are constructed test inputs, **not historical SEC observations**. The prototype and tests have **not yet been published**; this checkpoint must not be mistaken for a reproducible code release.

## Method and interpretation

Starting with date contrasts generated by the fixed security/exchange roster and matched-group estimator in Phase 208, require sorted unique canonical dates, finite contrasts, two disjoint pre-reform windows with at least three observed sessions each, and contiguous positions within the supplied series. Calculate within-window mean and ordinary least-squares slope against observed-session index, and placebo mean shift = later-window mean minus earlier-window mean. This is **descriptive only**: it does not adjust for serial dependence, validate source provenance, establish the treatment mapping, or identify a reform effect. Contiguous positions in the *supplied* dates do not prove exchange-calendar completeness; Phase 204/205 calendar checks are separately required.

A constructed 12-date linear sequence yielded a placebo mean shift of 6 and first-window slope of 1 per observed session; a constant series yielded a zero shift. These numbers are solely unit-test expectations.

## Critical unresolved threats

The large October 2025 placebo signal and September pretrends undermine a causal interpretation. Historical treatment assignment, SEC source authentication, fixed-roster coverage, exchange/calendar coverage, selection/weighting sensitivity, and serial/cross-sectional dependence-aware inference remain unverified. No new historical coefficients or figures were produced. Do not claim a causal effect.
